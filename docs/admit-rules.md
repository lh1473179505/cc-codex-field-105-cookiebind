# solaceacl admit rules

message_vpn, client and exchange select one nested publication policy in that order. A client or exchange registered elsewhere cannot be borrowed. permission is an exact case-sensitive member of the selected exchange permissions, and publish does not implicitly include subscribe or administrative operations.

topic is a concrete slash-separated destination beginning with one configured topic_prefix, including its slash boundary. Empty or dot-dot components, wildcards, NUL and more than topic_max_segments components are denied; normalization never repairs an invalid topic. Publication authorization is not subscription authorization, and wildcard subscriptions are not accepted as concrete destinations.

ttl_seconds is a JSON integer in the exchange's inclusive range. All fields enter HMAC in declared order without topic normalization. Active-key verification, iat no older than 600 seconds and no more than 60 seconds in the future, and an unused nonce are required. Denied publication leaves nonce and store unchanged.
